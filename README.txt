Category photos for the browse tiles.

Drop one square image in here for each category, named after its id:

  skincare.jpg    Natural Skincare & Bodycare
  herbs.jpg       Herbs, Tea & Natural Remedies
  food.jpg        Healthy Food & Drinks
  menstrual.jpg   Menstrual & Intimate Care
  ritual.jpg      Home, Ritual & Wellbeing
  living.jpg      Home & Living
  kids.jpg        Kids & Family

.png works too, using the same name.

A category with no file here keeps showing its line icon, so photos can be
added one at a time. Nothing in the code needs to change either way.

Recommended: square crop, 1000 x 1000 pixels or larger, consistent light and
background across all seven.
